POLE AGES - SHORT SUMMARY
=========================
(Full details and numbers: age_assignment_explanation.txt in this folder.)

SOURCE
  Alectra Utilities, 2027 Rebasing Application (EB-2025-0252), Exhibit 2A,
  Distribution System Plan, Figure 17 "2023 Wood Pole Age Distribution".
  A histogram of 105,262 real wood poles in the GTA, grouped into 5-year age bins.
  Used because Alectra serves the Toronto area.

AGE DISTRIBUTION
  We use Alectra's histogram directly (an "empirical" distribution), not a fitted curve.
  It has more young poles than old ones, with a long tail of old poles up to 85 years.
  To give a pole an age: pick a 5-year bin using Alectra's shares, then pick a
  whole-number age inside that bin (every age in the bin equally likely).
    - Poles of unknown age (5.7%) are left out.
    - The open "76+" bin is capped at 85 years (our assumption).

LINE SECTIONS (poles built together share an age)
  Utilities install a whole stretch of line at once, so neighbouring poles are often
  the same age. A "line section" = the poles between two junctions (a junction is a
  pole with 3+ spans). Each section gets one age; each pole in it gets that age
  +/- 2 years.

SPOT REPLACEMENTS (single poles swapped later)
  Over the years, single poles get replaced (failed inspection, car hit, storm).
  So 20% of poles ignore their section and get their own age from the histogram.
  Where 20% comes from: Alectra replaced about 0.77% of its poles per year
  (3,259 poles in 2021-2024). Averaged over the pole ages, that means about 20%
  of poles have been swapped since their section was built.

60-YEAR CAP (limitation)
  The fragility curves (Ma et al. 2020) only go up to 60 years. Older poles are
  treated as 60 for the lookups, which UNDERESTIMATES their failure probability.
  The real age is kept in age_true_years, and flag_age_capped marks these poles.

RESULT - FEEDER 1878 SUBURBAN
  Mean age 30.2 years (Alectra: 29.9). 344 line sections.
  250 spot replacements. 177 of 1,432 poles capped at 60.
  The feeder's histogram matches Alectra's within about 1 percentage point per bin.

SETTINGS (top of pipeline/asset_adapter.py)
  AGE_MODE = "alectra"   ALECTRA_RHO = 0.20 (spot replacements)
  ALECTRA_JITTER = 2     AGE_SEED = 2026 (same ages every run)
